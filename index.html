About

Your partner in productivity

Aisosa Cynthia Edobor has spent the last three years inside the operations of growing businesses — not just supporting them, but building the systems that keep them running.

She built the weekly reporting rhythms that kept business development, finance, and operations teams aligned. She managed calendars and inboxes that leadership actually trusted. She wrote the bids and proposals that won new clients. From booking travel and hotels to running errands and handling the small logistics no one else has time for — she covers the full range of what keeps a founder moving.

With a Bachelor's in Library and Information Science from the University of Benin and certification as a Virtual Assistant through ALX Africa, Aisosa brings disciplined documentation, information retrieval, and organizational structure to every role.

Her range includes meeting minutes, handover documents, brand activation materials, supplier compliance, executive social presence, and client acquisition. More than once, she has walked into a company with no systems and left it with structure that outlasted her.

She now works with founders, executives, and entrepreneurs who need someone who can hold their business together while they build it — based in Lagos, serving clients across the US, UK, Canada, UAE, and Australia.
